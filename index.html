Academic Profile

Bridging literary scholarship, environmental humanities, and language education.

Dr. Mackeus Nguni holds a PhD in African Literature from the University of Buea. His research and teaching bridge literary scholarship, environmental humanities, cultural studies, and language education. His academic journey is shaped by a sustained interest in African literary traditions, cultural identity, indigenous knowledge systems, and the relationship between human beings and their natural environment.

He approaches his career through two interconnected responsibilities: the production of knowledge through research, and the transmission of knowledge through university teaching.

Research and Specialisation

Theoretical Orientation

Dr. Nguni's principal area of specialisation is African Literature, with particular interest in the intersections of literature, culture, spirituality, and the environment. A significant part of his research examines how African writers represent nature beyond its physical and economic dimensions, as a space of cultural memory, spiritual significance, and ancestral relationships.

Ecocriticism

Examining the relationship between literature and the physical environment, paying attention to representations of nature, environmental consciousness, and ecological destruction.

Ethnocriticism

Investigating how myths, rituals, ancestral traditions, and indigenous beliefs inform literary representations of nature and ecological ethics.
